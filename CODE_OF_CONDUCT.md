# Code of conduct

texe is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Be considerate, specific, and open to correction.
- Make room for contributors with different experience and communication
  styles.
- Respect privacy and stop unwelcome contact when asked.
- Accept moderation decisions without retaliation.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained personal attacks, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, release channels, and project-related private communication. It
also applies when someone is representing texe in another public space.

## Reporting

Routine disagreements should be resolved in the relevant issue or pull request
when it is safe to do so. Report harassment, threats, privacy violations, or
other sensitive conduct privately through GitHub's
[report form](https://github.com/backmatter/texe/security/advisories/new).
Begin the report title with `Conduct report` so it is routed correctly.

Include links or screenshots, when safe, and describe the requested immediate
action. Reports are shared only with maintainers who need them for
investigation. If a report concerns a maintainer, request that another
maintainer handle it. Do not put sensitive conduct details in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates these expectations. Depending on severity and history, responses
may include a private warning, temporary participation restriction, removal
from a project space, or a permanent ban.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
conflicts of interest where practical. Retaliation against a reporter or
participant in an investigation is itself a violation. Appeals may be
submitted through the same private form and should be reviewed by a different
maintainer when possible.
